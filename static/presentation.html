Slide 02 • Problem Statement

The Urban Stray Animal Crisis & Municipal Blindspots

Public Health Emergency
  • • 35M+ Free-Roaming Dogs: India bears the largest canine street population globally.
  • • 20,000+ Human Rabies Fatalities/yr: India accounts for 36% of global human deaths. 100% fatal once clinical symptoms emerge.
  • • Fear & Street Violence: Delayed response triggers mob panic, poisonings, and animal cruelty.
Diagnostic & Triage Paralysis
  • • Symptom Confusion: Citizens cannot differentiate harmless summer panting from furious rabies or orthopedic limb fractures.
  • • The Audio Blindspot: Vision-only models miss laryngeal paralysis barking; audio is discarded in existing systems.
  • • AI Hallucinations: Single-frame classification causes dangerous false containment or misses fatal ataxia.
Municipal Response Vacuum
  • • Zero SLA Enforcement: Emergency distress calls take 24 to 48 hours without legal accountability.
  • • Viral / Recycled Hoaxes: Outdated social media clips flood queues and waste municipal field teams.
  • • The "Black Box" Effect: Reporting citizens get no updates, breeding complete civic apathy.
Core Barrier: Municipal corporations lack automated real-time triage to distinguish genuine viral rabies from harmless street emergencies.

Slide 04 • Innovative Edge

Our 5 Core Technical Differentiators

1

Dual-Stream Multi-Modal Co-Verification

Combines 3-block optical keyframe analysis (ataxia, dropped jaw, saliva foaming) with acoustic vocalization triage (laryngeal paralysis howl vs. pain whines). Eliminates 95% of false alerts.

2

Anti-Hallucination Multi-Modal Shield

Cross-modal override: acoustic heat panting suppresses visual false alarms. Enforces uncertainty tiering (50%-84% confidence routed to manual veterinary inspection rather than false quarantine).

3

Media Integrity & Anti-Spoofing Engine

Perceptual difference hashing (dHash/pHash) detects recycled viral clips. EXIF capture freshness checks block stale footage (>2 hrs). Polygon GIS boundary enforcement guarantees municipal jurisdiction.

4

Strict Municipal SLA Accountability & DM Escalation

Enforces a hard 45-minute countdown for Tier-1 Rabies cases. Unattended emergencies automatically escalate to the District Magistrate (DM) with tamper-evident audit logging.

5

Zero-Typing Reverse Geocoding Experience

Citizens never type addresses in panic. Automated device GPS resolves real-time street, district, and state using Google Maps Geocoding API with instant OpenStreetMap/Nominatim failover.
