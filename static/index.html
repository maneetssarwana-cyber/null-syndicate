Dog Safety & Bite Emergency First-Aid

Thirsty Panting (Harmless)

Clean tongue, clear watery saliva, dog resting in shade. Action: Leave a bowl of fresh clean water.

Suspected Rabies (Danger)

Thick ropey drool, jaw hanging open, drunken stumbling walk. Action: Keep 10m distance, report immediately.

Emergency First-Aid If Bitten:
  1. Wash immediately: Flush wound with running tap water and soap continuously for 15 minutes.
  2. Apply antiseptic: Apply Povidone-Iodine solution if available.
  3. Hospital: Visit nearest government hospital within 24 hours for Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP).
